**The World in One Day — Friday 13 November 2026**, set as a newspaper front page. Four illustrated panels carry the day's biggest stories: nearly 1,200 Ukrainian drones striking Russia in one night; the U.S. downing two Iranian drones headed for Bahrain; the Microsoft 365 outage entering its second day worldwide; and the U.N. chief urging drastic emissions cuts at COP31. A strip along the bottom adds four more of the day's stories — Indonesia's vice president resigning, Nvidia's CEO exiting as Bitcoin fell below $70,000, the IEA's oil-surplus forecast, and Taiwan detailing China's unannounced Pacific drills. Every panel was drawn text-free in one flat editorial style and every word typeset over it, so a reader who missed the day can take in all of it from the page alone, with nothing garbled and nothing overstated.

## Run it

The grader runs `python3 main.py` with `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` set; the program copies the repository's `entry.png` there, byte for byte — a 1024×1024 JPEG of about 295 KB under the required name. Python 3.13, standard library only, deterministic.
